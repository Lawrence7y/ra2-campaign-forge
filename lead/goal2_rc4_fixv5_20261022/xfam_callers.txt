METHOD
  1. gap-skipping linear sweep of .text with capstone: every instruction START is recorded,
     undecodable bytes are skipped 1 at a time (so data blobs do not desync the sweep).
  2. a call site counts only if the sweep put an instruction boundary at that address AND the
     decoded instruction is "call 0x<target>". This filters false E8 bytes inside immediates.
  3. EAX liveness: walk the CFG forward from call+5; stop at ret/jmp-out/call (callee clobbers eax)
     or at the first instruction that writes eax without reading it. Report READ if eax is read first.
